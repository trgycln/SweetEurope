<h2>What Is the Brix Value and Why Is It Relevant?</h2>
<p>The Brix value indicates the proportion of dissolved solids—mainly sugar—in a liquid and is measured in degrees Brix (°Bx). A value of 10 °Bx means that 10 % of the solution consists of sugar. In practice, this value enables exact calibration of syrups, fruit juices, and other sweet components used in cocktails.</p>

<h3>Flavor Consistency Across All Layers</h3>
<p>A constant Brix value ensures that a cocktail maintains the same sweetness from the first to the last layer. This is especially important when you prepare multiple servings in advance or offer seasonal special editions. Without this control, sweetness can vary from batch to batch, leading to dissatisfied guests and negative online reviews.</p>
